## ASO Strategy & Future Improvements

### Screenshot Optimization (High Priority)

See Screenshots section above. Screenshots are the most viewed element after the app icon.

### Competitive Differentiation

**Unique positioning vs competitors:**

- Activity Monitor: Tab-switching required, no consolidated view, no sparklines
- iStat Menus: Menu bar focused, subscription pricing, overwhelming for casual users
- Stats (free): Minimal menu bar stats, no dashboard, no process grouping
- htop/btop (terminal): Developer tools, no GUI, no GPU metrics on macOS

**Key differentiators:**

- Single-window dashboard with all metrics + history sparklines
- Per-app process grouping (helpers rolled up under parent app)
- Menu bar companion alongside full dashboard
- Self-monitoring footer (app tracks its own footprint)
- Designed for Apple Silicon / high-core-count machines

### Localization Priority (Future)

Consider localizing for markets with strong developer/power-user communities:

- Japanese
- German
- Chinese (Simplified)
- Korean

### Performance Metrics to Track

**App Store Connect Analytics:**

- App Store impression → product page views (measures icon/name appeal)
- Product page views → downloads (measures page conversion)
- Search terms driving traffic (validates keyword strategy)
